How to get the most out of this page

Every subject is organised chapter by chapter, and chapters with many topics are split into subchapters so you can drill down to exactly the area you need. Follow this simple routine to turn practice into marks:

  • Study first, then test. Finish a chapter from your notes or textbook, then open that chapter's MCQ test and practise 20–50 questions with negative marking applied exactly as in the exam.
  • Review every solution. Each question shows why the correct answer is right and why each wrong option is wrong. That is where the real learning happens.
  • Track your accuracy. After every test you get your score, accuracy, and time taken. Use that feedback to decide which chapters need another round before moving on.
  • Progress to full tests. Once your chapter accuracy is solid, take the full mock test with 180 questions under exam conditions to build speed, stamina, and confidence.
