About CEE MCQ

CEE MCQ started from a simple frustration. While preparing for Nepal's Common Entrance Examination, finding good chapter wise practice questions online was harder than it should have been. Notes were scattered across PDFs, Facebook groups, and outdated blog posts, and there was no single place to actually test what you knew, chapter by chapter, the way the real exam would test you. This site was built to fix that gap.

What CEE MCQ Actually Does

CEE MCQ is a free practice platform for students preparing for the CEE in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and MAT. Instead of long reading sessions, the site is built around active practice: chapter wise quizzes, subchapter-level drills for topics that need closer attention, and full length mock tests that simulate the real 200 question exam with negative marking and a timer. Every quiz tracks your score, accuracy, and time taken, so you can see exactly where you are improving and where you are not.

The Question Bank

The platform currently hosts over 56,000 original MCQ questions across all four subjects, each with a verified correct answer and a written explanation. Questions are sourced from the official CEE syllabus, past paper patterns, and standard reference textbooks used in Nepali medical and science education. Every question is structured to match the difficulty level and format of the actual CEE exam, with four options, one correct answer, and a solution that explains the reasoning behind the correct choice.

The question bank is not a scraped or repackaged collection from other websites. Each question was written or adapted specifically for this platform, reviewed for accuracy, and organised into chapters and subchapters that mirror the CEE syllabus structure. When students report errors through the in-app report feature or the contact page, those questions are reviewed and corrected. This ongoing review process has been one of the most important ways the question bank has improved over time.

How the Platform Was Built

CEE MCQ is built with Django (Python) and has been serving students since early 2025. The front end is designed to work on mobile phones, which is how most Nepali students access the internet. The platform grew incrementally: starting with a basic quiz engine, then adding chapter wise organisation, subchapter-level drilling, solved question sets with full explanations, a full length mock test simulator, progress tracking, and a blog with practical study guides.

Every feature on the site was added because students asked for it or because it directly helped with CEE preparation. There are no unnecessary sign-up barriers, paywalls, or features designed to collect personal data. The site uses cookies only for analytics (Google Analytics) and to display advertisements (Google AdSense) after explicit consent, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Who Built This

CEE MCQ is built and maintained by Ramu Pandey, who created the platform after going through CEE preparation and noticing how disorganized the available practice resources were. The experience of preparing for CEE, understanding its structure, and identifying which chapters and topics carry the most weight directly shaped how the platform is organised and what content is prioritised. The site is a solo, ongoing project rather than a large commercial product, which means it stays focused on what actually helps students revise rather than on unrelated features. Feedback from real students using the platform directly shapes what gets added or changed.

Why This Is Different From Other CEE Sites

Nepal's CEE prep space has several existing MCQ sites and resources. CEE MCQ is different in a few specific ways:

  • Chapter wise organisation: Questions are organised exactly like the CEE syllabus, so you can drill a specific weak chapter instead of doing random mixed practice.
  • Written explanations: Every question has a solution that explains the reasoning, not just the answer letter. This matters for learning, not just testing.
  • Free and open: No login required for any content. No premium tier. No paywall hiding solutions.
  • Original content: Questions are written or adapted for this platform, not scraped from other sites.
  • Practical blog: Study guides written from direct CEE experience, not generic advice.

Where This Is Going

CEE MCQ is actively maintained, and new chapters, subchapters, and blog content are added as the question bank grows. The long term goal is to make this the most complete free chapter wise CEE practice resource available to Nepali students, without paywalls or unnecessary sign-up barriers getting in the way of practice.

Questions or Feedback

If you spot an incorrect question, have a suggestion for a missing chapter, or just want to share how your preparation is going, you can reach out through the contact page. Most improvements to this platform have come directly from students pointing out what was missing.
